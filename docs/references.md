# Key references

A short working list. The full literature review belongs beside this file as `docs/literature_review.md`. Check details against the papers themselves before citing.

## Closest prior work

- Bransen, Robberechts, Van Haaren & Davis (2019). *Choke or Shine? Quantifying Soccer Players' Abilities to Perform Under Mental Pressure.* MIT Sloan Sports Analytics Conference. Separates choice from execution under contextual pressure.
- Tena & Tovar (2023). *Emotional Shocks and Performance: Evidence from the FIFA World Cup.* International Journal of Sport Finance. Passing after goals scored and conceded; execution only.
- Gauriot & Page (2018). *Psychological momentum in contests: The case of scoring before half-time in football.* Journal of Economic Behavior & Organization. Shots off the post as the counterfactual for goals.
- Gauriot & Page (2019). *Fooled by Performance Randomness: Over-rewarding Luck.* Review of Economics and Statistics. Lucky scorers get more minutes next match: a selection problem for any cross-match design.

## Hot hand and choice after success

- Gilovich, Vallone & Tversky (1985). The original hot-hand study.
- Miller & Sanjurjo (2018). *Surprised by the Hot Hand Fallacy?* Econometrica. Streak-selection bias.
- Attali (2013). Psychological Science. After a make, NBA players shoot again sooner, from further out, less accurately.
- Bocskocsky, Ezekowitz & Stein (2014). MIT Sloan. Controlling for shot difficulty.

## Latent-state models

- Ötting, Langrock & Maruotti (2021). Copula-based multivariate hidden Markov model for momentum in football.
- Ötting & Groll (2022). Regularised hidden Markov model for the "hot shoe" in Bundesliga penalties.
- Ashwood et al. (2022). *Mice alternate between discrete strategies during perceptual decision-making.* Nature Neuroscience. The GLM-HMM template for Phase 6c.

## Models for the machine-learning layer

- Mendes-Neves, Meireles & Mendes-Moreira (2024). *Towards a foundation large events model for soccer.* Machine Learning.
- Robberechts, Van Roy & Davis (2023). *un-xPass: Measuring Soccer Player's Creativity.* KDD.
- Fernández & Bornn (2020). *SoccerMap.* ECML-PKDD.
- Decroos et al. (2019). *Actions Speak Louder than Goals (VAEP).* KDD. Implemented in the `socceraction` library.
